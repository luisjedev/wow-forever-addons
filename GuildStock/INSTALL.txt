GuildStock 0.4.1 - Release
For World of Warcraft: Forever 1.60.1 (interface 16001)

INSTALLATION
Extract the GuildStock folder into your Forever client's Interface/AddOns folder.
The resulting path must be Interface/AddOns/GuildStock/GuildStock.toc.
Restart the game, enable GuildStock, and install the same current version on
all participating guildmates' clients.

OPEN GUILDSTOCK
Use /guildstock, the chest minimap button, or the native Professions shortcut.
Left-click the minimap button to open/close; drag it to change its position.

FEATURES
- Search profession materials, filter by profession, and save favorites.
- Find participating guildmates who have a material and see their quantities,
  primary professions, online status, and last-online information when available.
- Browse and search each participating character's shared inventory.
- Start a whisper to an online guildmate; you write and send the message.
- View dated offline inventory history and choose whether to show offline members.
- See combined known material quantities from your bags and personal bank.
- Choose which materials to share, or disable your own sharing in Settings.
- Automatically synchronize shared inventories with players in the same guild.
- Find recipe materials through chest shortcuts in the native Professions window.
- Mark materials useful for your learned professions in native bags and your
  character bank, with profession names in the item tooltip.
- Adjust window scale, opening view, minimap visibility, and material-hint size.
- Use automatic or manually selected interface language. Supported languages:
  English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian,
  Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Use /reload after changing
  language. Item names keep the game language; included translated names can
  also be used to search for hundreds of materials.

GETTING STARTED
Sharing is enabled by default. Review My inventory and Settings to choose what
you share. Visit your personal bank once to discover materials stored only there.
The starting catalog includes 589 materials across 12 professions and can grow
as you open profession windows. It is not exhaustive.

GOOD TO KNOW
Quantities include bound items and do not guarantee that items can be traded.
Offline inventories show last-known stock and can be out of date. Empty results
are not proof that nobody owns a material. Hints show known profession uses,
not learned recipes or skill requirements. Account/guild banks are not included.
Bag and bank hints target Blizzard's native interface.
The Forever beta client can delay synchronization or affect saved data. Broader
multiplayer and full-restart checks remain ongoing. Retail and Classic Era are
not supported targets.
