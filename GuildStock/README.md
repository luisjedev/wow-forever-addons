# GuildStock · Automatic synchronization preview 0.3.3

An in-development implementation of the [GuildStock design](PLAN.md), targeting **WoW Forever beta 1.60.1, build 70205, interface 16001**. The interface uses near-black bronze surfaces, ivory text, four top tabs, and a three-column material browser. All tabs share borderless content panels, table headings, rows and item icons. A yellow underline identifies the active tab; a narrow side marker and soft background identify selected filters, materials and characters. Search fields, the opening-view menu and small profession slots retain subtle outlines. Its palette and native favorite stars refine the earlier mockups. The catalog is partial. Automatic guild inventory exchange is implemented for testing; complete peer inventory reception and rendering have been observed on the local beta client. Independent verification of the remote display and cross-shard delivery remains pending.

Copy this folder into the client's `Interface/AddOns`, restart the client, and enable GuildStock. Open the movable window with `/guildstock`, its crate minimap button, or the crate shortcut at the bottom-right of the native Professions window, separated from the profession tabs. Click the shortcut again to close GuildStock. Left-drag the minimap button to reposition it. The interface follows the client language by default: English, Spanish (`esES`/`esMX`), French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Unsupported languages and missing translations fall back to English. In Settings → Interface → Language, select Automatic (game language) or an explicit language, then use `/reload` to apply it. This choice is saved per character and does not change the game language. Displayed item names keep the client language. Item searches also accept names in the supported languages, independently of the interface selection, for the 579 materials with bundled aliases. For example, searching `Copper Ore` can find `Mineral de cobre` in a Spanish client. Materials must belong to the bundled or locally discovered catalog; aliases do not add items or stock. Uncovered items remain searchable by their native name. The [data provenance and license](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) document the earlier-build source and its limitations.

## Available now

- **Materials:** search, All materials / Favorites, and a Used by sidebar with All professions first. My professions lists your learned primary professions before secondary professions; Other professions contains the remaining filters without duplicates. Empty groups are hidden. The groups update when your skills change, preserving the selected filter. Item icons and favorite stars identify materials. Below the selected material's name, consuming-profession icons show localized names on hover, matching My inventory. The detail table shows received inventories under Player, Skills, Units, Last online and Whisper. Online status comes from the current guild roster; an unavailable last-connection time is shown as Unknown. Each Whisper button rechecks online presence and opens a native chat draft without submitting it. When there are no player rows, it shows “No players found with this material.” without an icon. This describes the available results; synchronization restrictions appear in Settings. It never invents owners or counts.
- **Characters:** immediately after Materials, a searchable character list on the left and the selected character’s item icons, names and bag quantities on the right, using the same inventory rows as My inventory. Item search and the last synchronization age belong to the selected character. This lists other participating characters, not the entire native guild roster or your own character (shown in My inventory). Only complete records for the current guild qualify, including known empty inventories; the view fills when complete inventories arrive from compatible guild clients. A missing or partial observation never becomes an empty inventory.
- **My inventory:** a searchable table of discovered profession materials and actual bag quantities, without item or quantity hover tooltips. The table takes 70% of the available width; Not shared occupies the remaining 30%. Uncheck the native Share checkbox to exclude an item from guild sharing. It stays in the bag table with its quantity and a Not shared with guild indicator, and also appears in the right panel. Check Share again or use the right panel's native Share button to remove the exclusion. Rows and inner panels have no separator borders; profession icons retain their 24-pixel slots and wrap when needed.
- **Settings:** offline-member display preference, minimap visibility, opening view, window scale and interface language. Synchronization status is read-only and appears here, with no footer indicator or manual synchronization controls. Native send failures are never presented as active sharing.
- Player names in Materials and Characters have a circular race icon from the native guild roster. It uses one standard icon per race; unavailable race data or artwork shows a question mark. Profession entries share the panel background without individual separators; their section headings retain their gold lines.
- Bag events coalesce into complete observations. Missing, inaccessible, locked, inconsistent or not-yet-loaded bags preserve the previous observation. Scanning resumes after combat. A successful empty observation removes previous quantities. No bank is scanned.
- `GuildStockDB` preserves bag observations, complete guild inventory history, discovered materials, favorites, hidden item IDs and display preferences per character. Unsupported schemas stay untouched and use temporary runtime data. The beta's historical persistence limitation remains open.

Excluded items stay excluded across bag changes, including using the last unit and acquiring more later. The right panel keeps them available even at zero stock or while searching the left table. Exclusions preserve local bag observations, discovered profession uses and favorites. `addon.ShareableSnapshot()` creates a separate copy without excluded item IDs, quantities or bound counts; unsupported privacy data prevents that export. Any future inventory sender must build from this filtered copy at send time and replace the previous peer observation so excluded entries disappear. Privacy changes join the fixed five-minute batch. A transfer already in progress is cancelled if its content changes, and every data packet rechecks the filtered observation. Previously received quantities remain dated until the replacement arrives; excluded items are absent from that replacement.

Both inventory tables use **Material, Used by, Units** columns. Used by shows the catalog's known consuming-profession icons, including secondary professions, with localized names on hover over each icon. There is no tooltip for the whole cell. A dash means the profession has not yet been identified; it does not mean the object has no profession use.

The **bundled partial catalog** provides the same starting set on every installation: **589 material IDs, 1,083 material–profession associations across 12 professions**. These are the persisted discoveries collected in Forever beta **1.60.1, build 70205**, extracted on **October 3, 2026**. Only numeric item IDs and known profession keys were copied into `CatalogSeed.lua`; no inventories, counts, characters, preferences or other profile data are distributed. Extraction parsed a restricted data grammar without executing the saved file. This snapshot preserves the observed relationships; it is not a fresh audit of every recipe or an exhaustive Forever catalog, and it is independent of the 579 earlier-build multilingual aliases.

Materials shared by professions are counted in each applicable row. The common catalog is available without opening profession windows. Names and icons are resolved by the local client's item APIs; an uncached name may initially appear as an item ID. Quantities come only from that character's own bag observations. No owners or quantities are included in the seed.

The base merges into the character's catalog without replacing existing discoveries, associations, favorites, exclusions or settings. Unsupported saved schemas remain untouched and use temporary runtime data. Opening profession windows can still add new recipe discoveries (for Mining, use smelting), and accessible crafting-reagent items can add materials from bags. Local discoveries remain searchable at zero stock and may extend the common base. Inventory exchange does not exchange recipe catalogs or invent new profession associations.

All materials is the default opening view. The removed For my professions preference is migrated to All materials; existing favorites and other preferences are preserved.

Material and inventory lists reuse only the visible rows plus one buffer row. Materials keeps its sorted catalog and latest search per filter in memory, updating them when discoveries, item names, favorites or bag observations change. Item-loading notifications are grouped, and typed searches apply after a short pause. Selecting a material updates its detail without rebuilding the list.

`/guildstock diagnostics` optionally prints the build/interface, raw restriction/lockdown flags, prefix/send results, synchronization state, packet and completed-inventory counts, and learned professions locally. It does not print character names, GUIDs or the roster. No command is required for normal synchronization.

## Automatic guild synchronization

Install **0.3.3 on every participating client**, restart after installation, enable GuildStock and enter the same beta environment, ruleset, faction and guild. Each client waits for a fresh complete bag read, initialized guild membership and messaging permission. There are no commands or publication controls: login/reload starts discovery after a short random delay, then the Materials and Characters tabs show complete inventories received from peers. The initial exchange can take longer for large inventories or throttled clients. Version 0.3.2 defers discovery replies during the 30-second offer cooldown instead of discarding them; 0.3.1 could leave a newly ready client without the other participant's inventory. This reply is coalesced and does not add a periodic heartbeat.

The shared inventory contains **catalog materials in the current character's bags**, filtered through the Share checkboxes. Counts include bound items, with their bound counts retained in the protocol; these are observations of ownership, not certified exchangeable stock or offers to trade. Bank storage and other characters' bags are not read. Each peer reports only its own inventory and two accessible primary-profession slots. The common 589-material seed remains available regardless of communication permission.

The first pending change starts a **fixed five-minute window**. Further loot, crafting, removal, profession metadata or sharing changes do not extend that deadline. At the deadline the final absolute inventory replaces the previous one, including removal of items at zero or excluded from sharing. If the final content equals the last publication, no update is sent. Incomplete bag reads preserve the old observation and pause data transmission until a complete read becomes available.

There is **no periodic network heartbeat**. Known missing fragments/revisions cause bounded repair requests; opening the window can trigger discovery at most once a minute. World transitions and reloads rediscover online peers. If an entire update and its revision announcement are both lost, it cannot be detected while everyone remains idle: recovery waits for later discovery or an update. Online presence comes from the native roster, never from inventory age. Unknown last-online times remain Unknown. Version 0.3.3 stores complete peer inventories in **per-character saved history**, scoped to the native guild club ID. After login/reload, historical rows become visible as soon as current membership can be verified, including offline members; new synchronization refreshes them in the background with the existing timing. Hover a player name in Materials or Characters to see their full name and **Last synced X seconds/minutes/hours ago** above the cursor. The Characters footer shows the same age and updates each second while visible. This age is the time since the last complete inventory received from that character, not their last login; partial packets, roster updates and idle time never reset it. Stored sessions and presence do not authorize new messages. Temporary roster unavailability hides records without deleting them. Explicit member-removal events and confirmed local guild changes discard the affected history. Unknown saved schemas remain untouched.

All packets use **GUILD**, prefix `GuildStockS1`, protocol 1. Discovery and revision offers lead to a fresh request/response challenge before a new sender session can replace a previous one. Full snapshots are split, staged and committed atomically; older/duplicate revisions never add quantities. Requests and small challenge acknowledgements include session/token addressing in the payload; other recipients ignore acknowledgements not intended for them. Full inventory broadcasts are coalesced so one transfer can serve several waiting clients. Acknowledgements never generate acknowledgements. No addon WHISPER, visible-chat fallback or alternate service is used.

Conservative implementation limits (not measured Blizzard limits): at most 240 payload bytes per packet, two packets per second without catch-up bursts, four items per fragment, 1,400 items per snapshot, 350 fragments, 16 simultaneous reassemblies, 200 participating peer states and 128 queued control packets. Snapshot replies are coalesced to at most one start per 30 seconds; request responses are limited per sender to one per 30 seconds. Each missing transfer gets at most three request attempts, with a five-minute response/reassembly timeout. API send failures get at most three attempts with backoff; exhausted work is dropped and reported rather than retried forever. New gameplay/discovery events can trigger later attempts. Large-guild behavior requires further client testing.

Combat and chat messaging lockdown are checked before sending; unavailable lockdown/membership/privacy information fails closed. Version 0.3.1 uses the native send result: in Forever build 70205, `AreOutgoingAddonChatMessagesRestricted()` returned true while a direct GUILD send returned `Success`. That flag is retained in diagnostics but no longer prevents an attempt on its own. Settings explains interrupted or waiting states while the local catalog and inventory remain usable. An API `Success` means accepted for sending, **not** proof of remote delivery. Received rows require a complete validated peer snapshot. Full regional sender names are preserved and checked against accessible current guild membership; there is no realm suffix or layer routing.

## Test with several clients

1. Everyone installs the same **0.3.3 ZIP** and logs in. Your friend needs no commands, setup or remote-control access.
2. Open GuildStock. Look for each participating character in Characters and for their shared material counts in Materials. Compare actual bag quantities between the consenting players.
3. Change a shared quantity, wait five minutes from the first change, and check the final count on another client. Repeat with the final stack removed and with Share unchecked; old quantities should disappear after the complete replacement arrives.
4. Check Settings and `/guildstock diagnostics` if no data arrives. Install 0.3.3 on every participant: 0.3.0 incorrectly stops on the raw restriction flag, and 0.3.1 can discard discovery replies during its cooldown. Versions 0.3.1–0.3.3 use the same wire protocol, but each participant needs the update to fix its own reply handling. Compare actual send results and completed-inventory counts; `sync received` counts complete inventories, while the older probe counters belong to a separate test. An accepted send alone does not prove delivery. Do not interpret an empty list as proof that nobody has materials.
5. Repeat after reload and a world/shard transition. Native persistence, identity matching, delivery across shards, Windows/macOS rendering and real API throughput remain pending. Automated multi-client simulations do not certify them.

The older `/guildstock probe` remains an optional separate protocol-2 diagnostic with a 60-second window on each participating test client; it does not start normal synchronization and is not part of the installation workflow. No diagnostic command is needed by your friend.

## Recipe material shortcuts

The native Professions window shows a GuildStock crate button to the right of each fixed material name. Click it to open Materials with that exact item selected and its recorded guild owners and quantities. This also works for materials missing from your bags or the partial catalog. Previous material searches, Favorites and profession filters are cleared; the offline-members preference still applies. Repeated clicks keep GuildStock open. Clear or edit the search, or select a sidebar filter, to resume normal browsing.

Buttons follow recipe changes and do not craft or change allocations. Combat defers attachment and prevents activation. Currency, optional and multiple-choice reagent slots are not supported by this shortcut. Results still depend on participating guildmates' shared inventories; an empty result is not proof that nobody owns the material.

## Remaining local checks

Check multilingual searches for discovered materials in Materials, My inventory and Characters, including new or renamed items without aliases. Check translated tabs, profession filters, sharing controls, tooltips and status messages in each supported language, including a manual override that differs from the client language. Verify the choice after `/reload` and a full restart, then return to Automatic. Verify long labels fit at different window scales and that Cyrillic, Korean and Chinese glyphs render correctly. Automated locale checks cover key completeness, format markers, command spelling, Spanish aliases and English fallback; they do not validate native font rendering.

Compare several bag stacks, including bound and unbound copies, against the window. Loot, craft, trade, sell and remove the final stack; check reagent bags if available. Verify item names finish loading, a missing read retains its dated snapshot, combat recovery, scrolling, Escape and minimap dragging. Uncheck Share for an item, check its row indicator and right-panel entry after `/reload`, then restore sharing; repeat with zero stock and a scrolling list of excluded items. Confirm data, exclusions and minimap position survive `/reload` and a full restart without editing personal SavedVariables files.

The full catalog audit and real-network validation remain pending. Owner rows, primary-profession slots, online/offline filtering and native Whisper drafts now consume validated received data; native rendering and addressing still need a client test. No complete-roster absence is assumed from a temporarily missing entry. Record observed results and remaining limitations in [the compatibility log](../docs/BLIZZARD_API.md); offline checks do not complete that gate.
