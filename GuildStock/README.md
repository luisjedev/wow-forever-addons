# GuildStock · Automatic synchronization preview 0.4.2

<img src="Assets/Logo.png" alt="GuildStock supply chest" width="160">

An in-development implementation of the [GuildStock design](PLAN.md), targeting **WoW Forever beta 1.60.1, build 70205, interface 16001**. The interface uses near-black bronze surfaces, ivory text, four top tabs, and a three-column material browser. All tabs share borderless content panels, table headings, rows and item icons. A yellow underline identifies the active tab; a narrow side marker and soft background identify selected filters, materials and characters. Search fields, the opening-view menu and small profession slots retain subtle outlines. Its palette and native favorite stars refine the earlier mockups. The catalog is partial. Automatic guild inventory exchange is implemented for testing; complete peer inventory reception and rendering have been observed on the local beta client. Independent verification of the remote display and cross-shard delivery remains pending.

Copy this folder into the client's `Interface/AddOns`, restart the client, and enable GuildStock. Open the movable window with `/guildstock`, its chest minimap button, or the chest shortcut at the bottom-right of the native Professions window, separated from the profession tabs. Click the shortcut again to close GuildStock. Left-drag the minimap button to reposition it. The interface follows the client language by default: English, Spanish (`esES`/`esMX`), French, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Unsupported languages and missing translations fall back to English. In Settings → Interface → Language, select Automatic (game language) or an explicit language, then use `/reload` to apply it. This choice is saved per character and does not change the game language. Displayed item names keep the client language. Item searches also accept names in the supported languages, independently of the interface selection, for the 579 materials with bundled aliases. For example, searching `Copper Ore` can find `Mineral de cobre` in a Spanish client. Materials must belong to the bundled or locally discovered catalog; aliases do not add items or stock. Uncovered items remain searchable by their native name. The [data provenance and license](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) document the earlier-build source and its limitations.

## Available now

- **Materials:** search, All materials / Favorites, and a Used by sidebar with All professions first. My professions lists your learned primary professions before secondary professions; Other professions contains the remaining filters without duplicates. Empty groups are hidden. The groups update when your skills change, preserving the selected filter. Item icons and favorite stars identify materials. Below the selected material's name, consuming-profession icons show localized names on hover, matching My inventory. The detail table shows received inventories under Player, Skills, Units, Last online and Whisper. Online status comes from the current guild roster; an unavailable last-connection time is shown as Unknown. Each Whisper button rechecks online presence and opens a native chat draft without submitting it. When there are no player rows, it shows “No players found with this material.” without an icon. This describes the available results; synchronization restrictions appear in Settings. It never invents owners or counts.
- **Characters:** immediately after Materials, a searchable character list on the left and the selected character’s item icons, names and inventory quantities on the right, using the same inventory rows as My inventory. Item search and the last synchronization age belong to the selected character. This lists other participating characters, not the entire native guild roster or your own character (shown in My inventory). Only complete records for the current guild qualify, including known empty inventories; the view fills when complete inventories arrive from compatible guild clients. A missing or partial observation never becomes an empty inventory.
- **My inventory:** a searchable table of discovered profession materials and combined bag and character-bank quantities, with native tooltips on item icons and no quantity hover tooltips. The table takes 70% of the available width; Not shared occupies the remaining 30%. Uncheck the native Share checkbox to exclude an item from guild sharing. It stays in the inventory table with its quantity and a Not shared with guild indicator, and also appears in the right panel. Check Share again or use the right panel's native Share button to remove the exclusion. Rows and inner panels have no separator borders; profession icons retain their 24-pixel slots and wrap when needed.
- **Settings:** global item sharing, offline-member display preference, minimap visibility, opening view, window scale, interface language and native bag material hints. Synchronization status is read-only and appears here, with no footer indicator or manual synchronization controls. Native send failures are never presented as active sharing.
- Player names in Materials and Characters have a circular race icon from the native guild roster. It uses one standard icon per race; unavailable race data or artwork shows a question mark. Profession entries share the panel background without individual separators; their section headings retain their gold lines.
- Inventory events coalesce into complete observations. Missing, inaccessible, locked, inconsistent or not-yet-loaded container reads, and invalid native totals, preserve the previous observation. Scanning resumes after combat. A successful zero total removes previous quantities. Character-bank slots are read only during a bank visit to discover material IDs.
- `GuildStockDB` preserves inventory observations and known material IDs, complete guild inventory history, discovered materials, favorites, hidden item IDs and display preferences per character. Unsupported schemas stay untouched and use temporary runtime data. The beta's historical persistence limitation remains open.

In **Settings → Material view**, turn off **Share items with guild** to stop sharing inventory items for the current character. Sharing starts enabled for existing and new installations. Your local inventory and each item's selection are preserved: Share checkboxes become disabled and grey while retaining their checkmarks, and the right panel's Share buttons are disabled too. Hover either control for instructions to enable general sharing in Settings. Receiving guild inventories continues. Re-enabling restores the original per-item choices and shares current quantities through the normal batch.

Excluded items stay excluded across inventory changes, including using the last unit and acquiring more later. The right panel keeps them available even at zero stock or while searching the left table. Exclusions preserve local inventory observations, discovered profession uses and favorites. `addon.ShareableSnapshot()` creates a separate copy without excluded item IDs, quantities or bound counts; unsupported privacy/settings data prevents that export. Every outgoing own-inventory packet rechecks current sharing permissions. Excluding an item or disabling general sharing cancels the affected transfer immediately and schedules a complete replacement without the normal 30-second batch. An exclusion can use the last complete observation while bags are unavailable; disabling general sharing sends an empty replacement even without a prior observation. Combat, messaging permission, send cooldowns and delivery still apply. Previously received quantities remain dated until a replacement arrives. Other participants can relay the newer replacement, including an empty withdrawal, to returning members while its owner is offline. Sharing off suppresses your own item data; receiving and relaying other members' already-shared history remain active on GUILD.

Hover any item icon in Materials, Favorites, the selected material detail, My inventory, Not shared or Characters to see the game’s native item tooltip, including effects, requirements and vendor value when available. Content follows the game language and describes the item type, not a particular stack or the total value of shared quantities.

Both inventory tables use **Material, Used by, Units** columns. Used by shows the catalog's known consuming-profession icons, including secondary professions, with localized names on hover over each icon. There is no tooltip for the whole cell. A dash means the profession has not yet been identified; it does not mean the object has no profession use.

The **bundled partial catalog** provides the same starting set on every installation: **589 material IDs, 1,083 material–profession associations across 12 professions**. These are the persisted discoveries collected in Forever beta **1.60.1, build 70205**, extracted on **October 3, 2026**. Only numeric item IDs and known profession keys were copied into `CatalogSeed.lua`; no inventories, counts, characters, preferences or other profile data are distributed. Extraction parsed a restricted data grammar without executing the saved file. This snapshot preserves the observed relationships; it is not a fresh audit of every recipe or an exhaustive Forever catalog, and it is independent of the 579 earlier-build multilingual aliases.

Materials shared by professions are counted in each applicable row. The common catalog is available without opening profession windows. Names and icons are resolved by the local client's item APIs; an uncached name may initially appear as an item ID. Quantities come from the current character's bags and personal bank for material IDs already observed on that character. No owners or quantities are included in the seed.

The base merges into the character's catalog without replacing existing discoveries, associations, favorites, exclusions or settings. Unsupported saved schemas remain untouched and use temporary runtime data. Opening profession windows can still add new recipe discoveries (for Mining, use smelting), and accessible crafting-reagent items can add materials from bags. Local discoveries remain searchable at zero stock and may extend the common base. Inventory exchange does not exchange recipe catalogs or invent new profession associations.

**Known material IDs, not a catalog-wide count sweep:** `GuildStockDB.knownMaterials` is a per-character set shared by the live addon and SavedVariables. Accessible bag observations, item-count events and bank visits add IDs recognized by the material catalog or native crafting-reagent metadata. Ordinary equipment and other items outside that material classification never enter the set. The 589 catalog entries are not copied into it. New recipe/name data can classify previously observed items; discovering a recipe alone does not imply ownership.

Login/reload and world transitions refresh native totals for known IDs with `C_Item.GetItemCount(id, true, false, false, false)`. During a session, bag/bank observation differences and `ITEM_COUNT_CHANGED` mark affected IDs; bursts share one delayed scan, and unchanged IDs reuse their totals. Closing the bank reconciles IDs from the last bank observation without reading closed-bank slots. The native result already includes bags and is never added to a saved bank quantity. Observed IDs remain known at zero; zero stock is removed from displayed/shared inventories. Unsupported registry data is preserved, with discoveries kept in RAM instead.

**Initial bank coverage:** materials already present only in the bank remain unknown until a readable visit discovers them, unless a previous bag observation supplied their IDs. Once remembered, their totals can be queried after login without another visit. No separate bank quantities are saved or summed. Native count freshness after a full application restart and event coverage for all bank operations still need client verification.

All materials is the default opening view. The removed For my professions preference is migrated to All materials; existing favorites and other preferences are preserved.

Material and inventory lists reuse only the visible rows plus one buffer row. Materials keeps its sorted catalog and latest search per filter in memory, updating them when discoveries, item names, favorites or inventory observations change. Item-loading notifications are grouped, and typed searches apply after a short pause. Selecting a material updates its detail without rebuilding the list.

`/guildstock diagnostics` optionally prints the build/interface, raw restriction/lockdown flags, prefix/send results, synchronization state, packet and completed-inventory counts, and learned professions locally. It does not print character names, GUIDs or the roster. No command is required for normal synchronization.

## Native bag and bank material hints

Version 0.4.1 adds **Settings → Interface → Mark materials useful for my professions**, enabled by default and saved per character. In Blizzard's individual and combined carried bags and the character bank, known materials for your learned professions receive a small hammer with a thin black outline and transparent surroundings in the upper-right corner. Their native item tooltip adds **Useful for:** with the matching profession icons and localized names, including learned secondary professions. **Bag icon size** adjusts the hammer from 10 to 24 in steps of one, with a smaller default of 14. Size changes apply immediately outside combat and are saved per character, independently of the GuildStock window scale. Turning the option off immediately removes both additions. The feature works with GuildStock's window closed and independently of guild sharing.

Hints use the existing partial catalog and update when bags, learned professions or discovered uses change. They do not check learned recipes or skill requirements; an unmarked item may have an undiscovered use. Native quality/quest borders, stack counts and item actions remain intact. Search-filtered items have no badge. Hints are suspended during combat and resume afterward; inaccessible data is omitted. The bank updates while open, including page changes and searches. Account/guild storage and replacement bag addons are outside this feature's scope.

## Automatic guild synchronization

Install **0.4.2 on every participating client** and reload the UI after updating. There are no manual publishing controls. Current membership, a fresh complete local inventory (unless sharing is disabled), and messaging permission gate startup. Login/reload starts discovery after a 1–3-second readiness delay. Version 0.4.2 retries that announcement about 15 and 45 seconds later to recover from delayed member presence or lost initial messages, then stops. Combat/chat lockdown can defer these retries; native failures retain the existing bounded failure budget. Cached records appear as soon as their current guild membership can be verified; new complete observations replace them in the background.

The shared inventory contains **known materials in the current character's bags and personal bank**, filtered through the general sharing setting and Share checkboxes. A material has one total: 2 in bags plus 1 in the bank appears as 3. Counts include bound items and are observations of ownership, not certified exchangeable stock or offers to trade. The retained `bound` field counts confirmed carried bound units only. Account/guild banks, separate reagent-bank storage and other characters' local inventories are excluded. Two accessible primary-profession slots accompany each observation.

| Trigger | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| A new participant requests inventory | Send the latest complete shareable observation without waiting for the normal change batch; coalesce simultaneous requests. |
| Loot, crafting, quantity changes or profession changes | The first change starts a **fixed 30-second window**. Further changes do not move its deadline. If contents return to the published state, send nothing. |
| Quantity changes during a transfer | Finish the frozen complete revision, then publish the pending state when due. Ordinary changes cannot indefinitely restart a large transfer. |
| An item is excluded or sharing is disabled | Cancel the affected own transfer and prioritize a filtered/empty replacement. Never finish packets containing newly excluded items. |
| A participant logs in or rediscovers peers | Exchange compact version summaries for offline members, request only missing/newer records, and retain the owner's original observation time. |

**Offline history:** if a guildmate receives an update while you are away and its owner later disconnects, that guildmate can supply the newer record when you return. Multiple holders are limited to three candidates per record; one requested GUILD transfer can serve several readers. Live owners supply their own observations, and current membership must verify both holder and owner. Full replacements are committed atomically, including empty inventories and privacy withdrawals. A known newer owner announcement prevents a superseded stored copy from being relayed, including after reload. A relay is indirect information from a guild member, not a freshly authenticated message from the offline owner; no peer can recover an update that nobody received before logout or undo disclosure of an older copy already held elsewhere.

**Versions and persistence:** protocol 2 uses prefix `GuildStockS2`, an owner-generated revision persisted in `GuildStockDB.syncClock`, and per-character guild history schema 2. Revisions advance across reload and world transitions; transport sessions still require fresh challenge responses for direct owners. Schema-1 history is retained for display without inventing relay versions; each old record becomes relayable after a complete protocol-2 update. Unknown schemas remain untouched. The counter relies on native SavedVariables persistence; restoring an old backup or resetting saved data is not a certified version-recovery workflow. Protocol-1 clients (0.3.x) cannot exchange packets with 0.4.0: **update all participants**. Older stored rows can still contain bags-only observations until refreshed.

The Materials tooltip and Characters footer show **Inventory observed X seconds/minutes/hours ago**, using the owner's observation time. Receiving or forwarding a record never makes its contents look newly observed. Online presence comes only from the native roster, not inventory age. Unavailable last-online times remain Unknown. Verified offline history has no automatic age expiry and stays visibly dated; confirmed departures/guild changes discard affected records. Temporary missing roster data hides records without deleting them. Relaying uses the exact native guild member ID within the current guild; it never constructs a realm/layer identity.

All traffic, including targeted requests, challenges and history replies, uses **GUILD**. There is **no periodic network heartbeat**. Opening the window can trigger discovery at most once a minute; reload/world transitions also rediscover peers. If an entire update and its announcement are lost, recovery waits for later discovery or an update. Native Whisper only opens a chat draft and has no transport role.

Conservative implementation limits (not measured Blizzard limits): 240 payload bytes per packet, two packets per second without catch-up bursts, up to 1,400 items/fragments per snapshot, 16 direct reassemblies plus one history download, 200 participating transport peers, 2,000 history candidates, 16 queued history replies, and 128 queued control packets. Items are packed by actual byte length. Own snapshot starts are coalesced to one per ten seconds; challenge replies are limited per sender to one per five seconds and have queue priority. Initial direct challenges allow 90 seconds for control queues, followed by a 20-second inactivity timeout while assembling; history requests also retry after 20 seconds without progress. Transfers receive at most three request attempts. Native send failures receive at most three attempts with backoff; exhausted work stays quiet until a later event. Encoded native member IDs longer than 80 bytes are not relayed or truncated; direct observations still work. Large-guild traffic, native opaque-ID consistency across clients, and real latency need client testing.

Combat and chat messaging lockdown are checked before sending; inaccessible membership/privacy/permission data fails closed. Native send results remain authoritative in build 70205, where the outgoing restriction flag can be true while GUILD sends succeed. A send success means API acceptance, not remote delivery. Independent remote-display, cross-shard behavior and the new three-client relay remain pending native verification.

## Test with several clients

1. Everyone installs the same **0.4.2 ZIP** and logs in. Your friend needs no commands, setup or remote-control access.
2. Open GuildStock. Look for each participating character in Characters and for their shared material counts in Materials. Compare combined bag and character-bank quantities between the consenting players.
3. Change a shared quantity, wait 30 seconds from the first change (plus transfer time), and check the final count on another client. Repeat with the final stack removed and with Share unchecked; old quantities should disappear after the complete replacement arrives.
4. Check Settings and `/guildstock diagnostics` if no data arrives. Install 0.4.2 on every participant; 0.3.x automatic-sync protocols are incompatible. Compare actual send results and completed-inventory counts; `sync received` includes complete direct and relayed inventories. The older probe counters belong to a separate diagnostic. An accepted send alone does not prove delivery. Do not interpret an empty list as proof that nobody has materials.
5. Repeat after reload and a world/shard transition. A dated historical inventory survived two local reloads with 0.4.0; full-restart persistence, cross-client identity matching, delivery across shards, Windows/macOS rendering coverage and real API throughput remain pending. Automated multi-client simulations do not certify them.
6. With three clients, disconnect the reader, change the owner's stock, and verify the holder receives it. Disconnect the owner and reconnect the reader: the holder must supply the newer inventory with its original age. Repeat after a holder reload, with an empty inventory and with sharing disabled. An older holder must not restore withdrawn quantities.

The optional `/guildstock probe` diagnostic listens for five minutes and repeats its request every 15 seconds until a matched acknowledgement arrives (at most 20 requests). Run it on the first client before asking the second to start; `/guildstock diagnostics` reports `probe remaining` in seconds and `probe requests`. Check both clients while their windows are active, and record the remaining time alongside `received`, `confirmed`, and `unmatched`. Repeating the command during an active window preserves its counters and deadline. A zero result without verified overlap is inconclusive. The diagnostic uses GUILD and the existing protocol-2 probe format, independently of automatic inventory synchronization; ordinary installation requires no diagnostic command.

## Recipe material shortcuts

The native Professions window shows a GuildStock crate button to the right of each fixed material name. Click it to open Materials with that exact item selected and its recorded guild owners and quantities. This also works for materials missing from your bags or the partial catalog. Previous material searches, Favorites and profession filters are cleared; the offline-members preference still applies. Repeated clicks keep GuildStock open. Clear or edit the search, or select a sidebar filter, to resume normal browsing.

Buttons follow recipe changes and do not craft or change allocations. Combat defers attachment and prevents activation. Currency, optional and multiple-choice reagent slots are not supported by this shortcut. Results still depend on participating guildmates' shared inventories; an empty result is not proof that nobody owns the material.
